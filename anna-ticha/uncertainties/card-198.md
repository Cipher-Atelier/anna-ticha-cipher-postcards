## U01

Position 20, `L01P020`, group `L01G05`: first unknown in `??esni`. The word proposal `dnešní` would require d/G06. A G06-like overwritten form remains an alternative to an unclassified overwritten form or touching signs. A short upper stroke, diagonal stem, middle crossing and lower hook suggest G06, but dense lower-left crossings and contact with position 21 prevent whole-form certification. **UNKNOWN remains.** Context `[530,963,658,1048]`; approximate focus `[540,981,560,1020]`.

## U02

Position 21, `L01P021`, group `L01G05`: second unknown in `??esni`. The proposal would require n/G14. One low multi-humped G14-like form competes with several touching low shoulder forms. ## U03

Position 79, `L03P018`, group `L03G03`: `budejovi?` → contextual `Budějovic`, requiring c/G19. Alternatives are a G09-like bowl with an upper bend (a), a pointed G19-like loop (c), or an unclassified narrow-loop form. A long diagonal borders a narrow round bowl; upper continuation and lower closure do not resolve the distinction. This is not a newly established capped c. **UNKNOWN remains.** Context `[265,1077,450,1161]`; focus `[403,1098,425,1133]`.

## U04

Position 184, `L07P014`, group `L07G03`: `vzpom?n` → contextual `vzpomeň`. The phrase suggests e, and the final n softening is editorial. Retained graphical alternatives are a low G05/G12-like form with an upper mark or a low form plus unrelated ink, possibly from the preceding line. Those key families produce y/u, not the desired e. The shallow hook/bowl and upper oblique spot do not establish complete form or ownership. **UNKNOWN remains.** Context `[255,1279,436,1352]`; focus `[359,1304,379,1329]`.

The clear upper loop and open uneven lower-right trace permit both G20-like upper/lower loops (p) and a G02-like upper loop with an open right shoulder (r). `rripravuje` records the latter letter alternative, not an equal linguistic probability. **UNKNOWN remains.** Context `[536,1391,788,1477]`; focus `[543,1401,565,1445]`.

## Editorial operations

### E01

`dlohych` → `‹dlouhých?›` inserts u between o and h. This is separate from the seven UNKNOWN positions; no extra source sign for u is confirmed. An omission while writing is a possible explanation, not an established fact. The literal retains `dlohych`.

### E02

`vasni ve` → `vášnivě` and `vasni / vych` → `vášnivých` join separated word parts and restore diacritics without adding letters. Reader word boundaries remain editorial.

### E03

Diacritics, capitalization and punctuation are restored for reading, not as proof of each overhead mark's value. Historical `odejeti`, `zlíbati` and `do plesu` remain.

### E04

`ve svinech` is displayed as `Ve Svinech`, a working place-name interpretation without identifying a particular municipality. `Jankové` and `Pepouše` are name/nickname readings, not established historical identities. `Budějovic` remains marked because of U03.

### E05

Russian expresses the interpreted sense: `mé poručení` becomes «мой привет», and «о тебе» supplies the implied object of the closing `vzpomínajícího`. These are translation choices, not extra ciphertext signs. «Пожалуйста» renders proposed `laskavě`.

## What would change the status?

A discriminating source or an explicitly qualified assessment of the original could support a change. Enlarging the same JPEG adds no evidence. Until then the proposals remain marked and all original masks, keys, literals and historical tests stay unchanged.
