## Dictionary-supported interpretations

- **`sanici`** is retained without letter repair. The official [SSJČ entry for *sanice*, sense 3](https://ssjc.ujc.cas.cz/search.php?heslo=sanice&hsubstr=no) supports sleigh riding/sledding. This supports the translation, not independent glyph truth. `vaši` may refer to the group's outing while `jsi` addresses the recipient. Locator: `C199.L02.T17–T22`, bodies 41–46.
- **`výboři`** preserves the older plural referring to committee members, attested in the official [PSJČ entry for *výbor*](https://bara.ujc.cas.cz/psjc/search.php?heslo=v%C3%BDbor&pocet_karet=50&zobraz_cards=cards&zobraz_ps=ps). Only diacritics are restored to `vybori`; it is not replaced with `výbory`. Locator: `C199.L04.T18–T23`, bodies 90–95.

## Other editorial and translation decisions

`vplesu` becomes `V plesu` by spacing and capitalization; it is not replaced with `na plese`. `premlou vati` becomes `přemlouvati` by joining groups and restoring diacritics, without base-letter insertion or deletion. Historical `jíti` is retained.

`Bez [tebe?] by mne tam nebylo nic bavilo` retains the older conditional syntax. Russian conveys that the writer would not have enjoyed the ball without the recipient; it does not say that he attended and was bored. Russian `[купить?]` represents proposed `[ke?] koupi`, whose two unknown source positions precede the already read `koupi`. Pepouš remains a textual nickname, not an established legal identity.
