## Meaning and editorial choices

The writer thanks the recipient warmly for a dear, beautiful card or note, says things are now going better, and promises a similar report in the next few days. An affectionate closing offers a warm, long kiss and addresses “my sweet beloved.” The precise adjective divided across lines 6–7 remains unresolved. *Upřímnou*, “sincere,” is a linguistic conjecture requiring the unresolved cluster to supply `m`.

Accent-only normalization of the clear portions would include `drahý`, `krásný`, `lístek`, `tisícerý`, `dík`, `mně`, `již`, `lépe`, `nejbližších`, `zašlu`, `zprávu`, `vřelou`, `sladká`, `tvého` and `vzpomínajícího`. Joining the line-final hyphens is an additional editorial operation. `Pepouše` is a proposed capitalized, accented form of the final word, compatible with the familiar-name interpretation used elsewhere; it does not identify a legal name or prove common authorship of different cards. No accepted base letter is replaced to obtain this interpretation.

The words about doing better do not identify an illness, its cause or a particular event. The affectionate formula does not establish the recipient's reply, a physical kiss or the people's relationship status.
