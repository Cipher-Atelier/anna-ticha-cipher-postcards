## Result

The preferred literal supports an affectionate note describing slow recuperation, limited earlier walking in a room, recent days back in bed and continued weakness. It reports a physician’s wish that the writer take leave and go “na venek” home before taking up service or duty. The journey, leave and entry into duty are not stated as completed or even scheduled. A prior period of the same employment or duty is not established. The unusual “na venek” may suggest out to the country or out of town, but no precise destination is fixed.

This is an interpretive reading of a noisy literal, not a complete decipherment. The proposed fluent Czech uses 10 changes to already mapped base letters and five conjectural UNKNOWN fills, all kept outside the first output. No diagnosis, illness/injury cause, military status, occupation, verified person or relationship follows. The year remains disputed between 1901 and 1904, and the month is unresolved. This card supplies no secure chronological anchor.

## Cautious English meaning

The main region warmly thanks Anduličko for a dear greeting or wishes and expresses affection. The writer describes recuperation proceeding slowly, earlier limited walking around the room, and more recent time spent again in bed. “Approximately three times” does not establish three walks per day, three circuits, a distance or an exercise prescription. The doctor wants leave and a homeward journey before taking up duty; leave being granted, the advice being accepted, and the trip occurring are not established. The writer hopes for faster recovery and further dear written lines. The closing “Pepouš” is a familiar signing-form normalization, not a verified identity. “Your” depends on retaining the marked four-body span and repairing its first letter; neither emphasis nor deletion is selected.

The separate margin plausibly says “Warm thanks also for the nice little note/card.” It needs the unknown fill ?ik→dík and the known-letter repair lystek→lístek. The exact written medium and postscript status are not proved.
