## Main finding

Card 202 gives an affectionate Czech reassurance addressed to Anička. The writer says corresponding with her is welcome, explains delayed replies by tiredness and receiving her notes late, and says his feelings are unchanged. A separately oriented margin asks to whom she promised that she would go to a ball. Another line appears to complain of a painful head. The closing contains “pepouse,” naturally interpreted as the inflected affectionate signature Pepouše.

The question about the ball concerns the person to whom the alleged promise was made. It does not say that this person would accompany her or establish an actual promise or attendance. These are the writer’s statements and a conservative interpretation of the literal, not independent historical verification.

## Source date and address

Two independent source readers agree on the separate inscription “16. / I. 1904.”, or 16 January 1904. They were not given the catalogue date. The address-side reading is “Velectěná Slečna / slečna Anna Tichá, / učitelka v / Žirovnici / u Počátek.” This gives a mailing destination and professional address wording; no school, civil identity or residence is established. Written date and postal impressions have not been assigned dispatch/receipt functions.

Three records are unassigned: one new graphical form without a key value and the two obscured spans. This is approximately 99.3–99.6% source coverage, not a plaintext accuracy score.

Five adjudicated graphical readings retain medium confidence and alternatives: A05.O035, A08.O020, A09.O018, A09.O019 and C01.O035. A09.O018 is the unmapped new form. Three marked forms match the pre-existing broad G10_CAP family. None of the marks qualifies for strict V2 normalization; no unknown position is added by V2, and primary and secondary outputs are identical. No new value, accent rule, numeric escape, null deletion or linguistic source repair was introduced.

## Interpretation and repairs kept separate

The broad reassurance is coherent. A separate Czech review finds strong syntactic support for interpreting A→B, because A ends with “nebo okolnost” and B begins “ze …”. That is a linguistic reading hypothesis, not proof of the material traversal. C resembles a closing; D contains additional remarks. Their order is not fixed by this test.

Several smooth interpretations need changes to already resolved letters. None has been applied to the first literal:
- vzdit → vzdyt, interpreted as “vždyť”
- pouzedobe → pouze tobe, interpreted as “pouze tobě”; “I live only for you” therefore remains conjectural
- nezdy → nekdy, interpreted as “někdy”
- od - / vovedeti → odpovedeti, interpreted as “odpověděti”; joining the line break alone is insufficient
- domnemky → domnenky, interpreted as “domněnky”
- neoduvodneme → neoduvodnene, interpreted as “neodůvodněné”
- hlainky → hlavinky requires inserting v; the alternative hlavičky additionally changes n to c before the caron
- steliti → sdeliti, interpreted as “sděliti”

The full review records accent-only, spacing, capitalization and actual letter repairs separately. They are linguistic conjectures, not accepted source corrections.

## Unresolved records

A09.O018: A distinct short-crossed-foot graphical form has no accepted key value. The context “?raci” suggests “prací,” but p remains a conjectural fill.
B04.O011: An over-inked one- or two-body span remains between “drahe” and “hlainky.” It is not silently deleted or moved inside the latter word to supply its missing v.
D03.O001: An over-inked one- or two-body span precedes “nes.” “Dnes” is plausible, but “a dnes” or “i dnes” also fit two-body hypotheses. The opening is unresolved.

The literal phrase “nyni v dobe vanocni a velikonocni” combines “now” with Christmas and Easter seasons. Its temporal awkwardness is preserved. It could loosely describe recurring busy seasons or reflect an unresolved textual issue; this test does not decide. It does not replace the separately read date or identify an occupation. The painful-head statement supports no medical diagnosis.

The signature Pepouše does not establish a full name, identity or physical authorship. The recipient’s original note is unavailable here, so her own wording, feelings and actual promises cannot be reconstructed from the writer’s assertions.

These are mechanical checks, not independent proof of historical correctness, exact segmentation or a complete unique cipher model. All source and linguistic reviews are AI-assisted. No external human expert validation is claimed.
