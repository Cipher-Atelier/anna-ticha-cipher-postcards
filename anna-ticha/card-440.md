## Editorial interpretation

The phrase about a dear letter crossing a note sent to zirovnice has a connected Czech grammatical frame. Žirovnice is the proposed place-name reading after capitalization and diacritics. A following first-person promise to answer as soon as possible gives the passage substance beyond its greeting and closing.

The message returns wishes for happy and merry holidays. The text itself does not identify a particular holiday. Its affectionate closing has a partly unresolved nickname.

## Uncertainties

The second literal is a post-test learned replay. It is not a new held-out test and does not replace the first 178/196 result.

Before this card was selected as a held-out target, preliminary source inspection had already noted opening and closing symbol sequences shared with card 441. The interior correspondence passages therefore provide the stronger independent evidence.

The first literal mescislnekra?e and the later mescislnekrate remain malformed. The proposed sense “countless times” requires changing resolved m to n as well as editorial diacritics; it is not an accepted correction.

The closing pepou? retains an unknown final source unit in both outputs.

## Method

The first result used the 23 assignments developed before this card was decoded. A later replay added one learned mapping for an unbarred form of t, raising coverage from 178 to 188 of 196 source positions. Both outputs are shown so that the improvement is not mistaken for part of the original prediction.
