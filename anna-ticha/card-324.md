## Meaning and editorial distinctions

With the editorial repairs below, the left strip can be interpreted as thanking “dear Andulka” for a lovely note and saying the writer still does not know when he will be able to go home. The intervening office statement and final “I continually think of you” need only accents and capitals. `V urade`, interpreted as `v úřadě`, supports being at an office or institution from morning until evening; it does not identify an employer, government department, profession or military service. The possible journey is not an agreed travel plan.

The bottom block is interpretable as “your Pepouš” looking forward greatly to a dear letter and kissing the recipient countless times. The later signing form can be the third-person subject of `tesi` and `liba`; no first-person endings are inserted. This expresses anticipation of a letter, not proof that it was later written or received.

Several literal sequences under the existing key remain distinct from the interpretation:

| Literal | Proposed editorial spelling | Operation |
|---|---|---|
| `diz` | `dík` | Replace accepted open-hook `z` with `k`, plus accent. |
| `lystek` | `lístek` | Replace accepted `y` with `i`, plus accent. |
| `noci` | `moci` | Replace accepted two-shoulder `n` with `m`. |
| `dumu` | `domů` | Replace accepted second `u` with `o`, plus final accent. |
| `nasmirne` | `nesmírně` | Replace accepted `a` with `e`, plus accents. |

The saved literal does not apply these changes. The thanks, lovely-note and travel interpretations depend on the first four repairs; the great-anticipation interpretation depends on the last. Three initial contextual guesses (`dik`, `moci`, `domu`) were corrected after source comparison. The fifth body in `vzpominam` has dense entrance ink but remains compatible with the accepted single-shoulder `m`; no extra shoulder is supplied from language. Familiar address and signing forms do not establish civil identities, residence, a completed trip or common authorship with another card.
