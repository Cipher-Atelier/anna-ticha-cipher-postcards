## Editorial interpretation

The proposed reading thanks the addressee for two notes. In the first, a seventh, dearly loved little angel seems to smile among six angels. The writer then asks what mood dictated the second note and expresses little inclination to dance. Exact wording, including the affectionate diminutive, remains incomplete.

A later passage appears to say that a waltz is not needed as a reminder, because the writer thinks of the addressee constantly. The fragment ?ofi n sk?ho does not securely identify a composer, place, title or person.

The card’s inscription is 14 days earlier than card 453, which may refer to a note sent 14 days previously. That possible connection is a contextual lead rather than an established exchange.

## Uncertainties

The secondary reading still leaves 60 of 298 positions unknown. The affectionate narrative does not resolve them.

The literal nelreba is preserved. Reading it as netřeba, “there is no need,” changes resolved l to t as well as adding a caron to r.

A smooth Czech version also needs editorial diacritics, spacing and uncertain word completions. Neither literal is a fully verified transcript.
