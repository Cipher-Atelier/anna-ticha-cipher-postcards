## Editorial interpretation

The opening sends a warm kiss to “Anouši,” an affectionate form of address. The following z usti may mean “from Ústí.” The picture’s printed caption reads “Ústí n. Orlicí,” although this does not establish where the writer was.

The partly readable body suggests that the writer was unwell on Sunday and is departing only today at noon. The next clause remains unclear: its central word is ?ystan? in the saved output. A later image review suggests “I will remain,” but that alternative has unresolved signs of its own.

The writer appears to expect a dear note that evening in pra?e, plausibly Prague, and promises to reply tomorrow. The closing contains a remembering or thinking-of phrase and the nickname pepouse. The three unknown signs after tisic may complete “a thousand kisses,” but that remains a conjecture.

The nearby ? u?ade can plausibly refer to an office, but the unresolved verb leaves the action unclear.
