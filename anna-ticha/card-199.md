## English translation

An interpretive translation of the Czech reader layer; the same content uncertainties remain linked below.

Warm thanks for your dear note. I was jealous about your sleigh outing until I found out who you had gone with.

I did not go to the ball, although members of the committee came to persuade me at the very last moment, on Saturday evening. The ball turned out beautifully. There were many [‹ladies I knew?›](uncertainties/card-199.md#u02) [‹there?›](uncertainties/card-199.md#u01). But I had no wish to amuse myself in their company. Without [‹you?›](uncertainties/card-199.md#u03), nothing there would have amused me.

On Monday I shall go to the Rudolfinum for the third concert of the [‹journalists?›](uncertainties/card-199.md#e02). I had wanted to go to the second concert yesterday, but there were no tickets left [‹to buy?›](uncertainties/card-199.md#u04). I am looking forward to this concert. The [‹performance?›](uncertainties/card-199.md#u06) by Ševčík’s school is said to be [‹magnificent?›](uncertainties/card-199.md#e03).

Pepouš, forever yours, sends a thousand [‹passionate?›](uncertainties/card-199.md#u07) kisses. He wishes his [‹sweetheart?›](uncertainties/card-199.md#u08) a good time, [‹but?›](uncertainties/card-199.md#e04) asks her not to forget that she belongs [‹exclusively?›](uncertainties/card-199.md#e05) to [‹Pepouš?›](uncertainties/card-199.md#u09).

## Editorial interpretation

The literal supports a connected account of thanks for a note, jealousy before learning who accompanied the addressee, and the writer’s absence from a ball despite attempts to persuade him to attend. The exact noun sanici remains uncertain. The message goes on to plan a Monday visit to the Rudolfinum for a third concert after finding no tickets for the second concert the previous day.

The separate region beginning skoly sevcikovy can be interpreted as a continuation referring to Ševčík’s school. Joining the two regions in this order is a linguistic interpretation; the frozen source transcription did not establish a cross-region reading order.

The inscription reads 26 January 1904, a Tuesday. If it dates composition and “Monday” means the next Monday, the proposed sequence points to 25 January and 1 February. The 25 January issue of Národní listy announces that day’s second concert of the Association of Czech Journalists at the Rudolfinum. The 31 January issue announces its third and final concert for Monday 1 February, with a group from Ševčík’s master school performing again. The linked primary newspaper pages match the dates, numbering, venue, organisation and school.

These notices were found after the fixed-key test. They corroborate the proposed reference but are advance announcements: they do not prove that the concerts occurred as advertised, that the writer attended, or who the writer was.

## Uncertainties

curnalistu is not literal žurnalistů. That interpretation changes resolved c to z before adding Czech diacritics.

Other proposed repairs exceed unknown completion: velkoleba → velkolepá changes b to p; avaak → avšak changes a to s; vyradne → výhradně inserts h.

A later source-only audit did not authorize those lexical repairs. It retained the sequences behind curnalistu and avaak, allowed h/b rather than p at the disputed velkoleba position, and did not establish an extra h in vyradne.

The displayed LEFT_CCW then RIGHT_CW order is an indexing convention. A coherent continuation does not settle source reading order.
