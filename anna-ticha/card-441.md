## Editorial interpretation

The proposed sense begins with affectionate thanks for two lovely notes and a promise to send a letter on Friday. A later expression is compatible with “this time two years ago,” but its preceding verb contains a resolved-letter anomaly. The card does not establish what past event might be intended.

The saved vzponi followed by mas joins to vzponimas. Standard vzpomínáš, “you remember,” requires two resolved-letter changes: n to m and m to n. A post-test reviewer shown only the relevant original image regions and neutral references confirmed all nine original classifications. That source audit provides no basis for repairing the two letters and does not establish the historical cause of the anomaly.

A separate AI reviewer given only the literal found a plausible affectionate Czech structure. Proposed readings involving duchu, “in spirit,” and vřelých, “warm,” would each require an unknown position to stand for ch. A CH unit exists in the broader key, so such completions are structurally possible, but these positions remain unassigned. The closing ve?ne admits at least věčně, “forever,” and věrně, “faithfully.”

The separately inspected address names Anna Tichá, describes her as a teacher, and gives Žirovnice near Počátky. This is address evidence, not a cipher-derived biography. The literal pepous can be normalized to Pepouš without identifying the sender.
