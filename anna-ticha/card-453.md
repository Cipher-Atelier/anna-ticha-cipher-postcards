## Editorial interpretation

The partly resolved Czech suggests an affectionate complaint about waiting every day for a promised letter. The following phrases ask whether the writer has given offence and possibly whether the addressee is ill. A note sent some days before and the hope of an answering letter connect the message.

A later image-only review interpreted the underlined pair rendered yi by the fixed key as the conventional numeral 14. This is a separate source interpretation, not a revision of the saved literal.

The inscriptions on cards 329 and 453 are exactly 14 calendar days apart. This makes card 329 a possible referent for the earlier note, but neither that connection nor a general rule for unencrypted underlined numerals has been established.

## Uncertainties

The possible illness, the exact wording of the questions and much of the closing depend on unknown completions.
