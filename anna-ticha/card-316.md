## Partial literal

[HCPortal record 1635, pc_hcp_316](https://crypto.hcportal.eu/dashboard/cryptograms/1635) supports this proposal:

```text
jedu prave domu.
boli mne hlavicka, jeste vice vsak srdecko po tobe,
t[y/i] ma cista lilie! nescislne pozdrv[y/i] a pusu [U2]
dopis co nejdrive,
```

The first three lines are the main lower block. The last displayed line is the separate upper-right note, placed after the block editorially; this does not establish its writing chronology. Both `[y/i]` records represent one proposed body with two existing class candidates, G05 and G07. G05 is preferred in both, but G07 remains possible. `[U2]` is the faded closing with unresolved segmentation and classes; it does not denote a known number of missing letters. Whether it mixes ordinary handwriting and cipher is also unresolved.

There are 102 invariant proposed mapped bodies across 24 contextual groups, using 20 existing classes, plus the two unresolved one-body class choices and U2. All invariant class values here emit one letter. These are ledger counts, not a whole-card body count or accuracy measure. No capped-ch class or secondary V2 transformation is applied.

## Meaning and necessary qualifications

The clear opening says “I am just going home.” The writer then mentions an aching head and a little heart that aches or longs even more for the recipient. The affectionate comparison to “my pure lily” follows if the first y/i choice is read as `y`, giving `ty`. This is an interpretation of the proposed text, not a medical diagnosis or confirmation of a particular journey.

The next passage appears to offer many greetings and a kiss. However, the saved sequence is `pozdrv[y/i]`: no distinct G09 = `a` was found between the proposed `r` and `v`. The fluent Czech `pozdravy` would require both selecting `y` and inserting an otherwise unestablished `a`. It is a linguistic conjecture, not the literal. A possible merged contour cannot be treated as proof of that missing body. The closing remains unknown, so no sender name is supplied.

The separate note reads `dopis co nejdrive`, approximately “a letter as soon as possible.” Its elliptical wording does not by itself decide whether this is a request or a promise.

Accent-only normalization of selected clear words would give `právě`, `domů`, `bolí`, `hlavička`, `ještě`, `více`, `však`, `srdéčko`, `tobě`, `má`, `čistá`, `nesčíslné` and `nejdříve`. Accents, capitals and a fluent repair of the greeting are not part of the literal. No complete normalized Czech paragraph is presented because that would conceal the unresolved wording.
