## Cautious interpretation

English: Your “pepous” sends you a [v?elou: unresolved adjective] kiss.

A natural one-letter language conjecture is vřelou, “warm/affectionate.” A two-letter es expansion gives veselou, “cheerful,” which is less conventional in this greeting but grammatically possible. Neither is selected. These are illustrative linguistic possibilities, not an exhaustive candidate list or proof that body count equals orthographic letter count. The cautious result leaves the adjective unresolved.

An optional one-body normalization is:
Vřelou pusu ti
posílá tvůj Pepouš.

The alternative two-body illustration is:
Veselou pusu ti
posílá tvůj Pepouš.

Neither normalized version replaces the source or literal. No known base letter is changed. All four spaces, both physical lines and the terminal point are preserved. Optional editorial changes are v→V at the start; posila→posílá; tvuj→tvůj; pepous→Pepouš; and the visibly conjectural unknown expansion ř or es. The base and caron in ř are both conjectural. The full character/intervention ledger and byte-identical diplomatic copy are preserved with the research evidence.

The wording is a written affectionate formula, not evidence of an actual physical kiss. “Pepouš” is a plausible familiar-name spelling only. No legal given name, historical person, relationship status or identity is established.
