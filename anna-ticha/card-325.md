## Meaning and editorial limits

The opening sends a warm greeting to “dear Andulka” from `zizkovske posty`, interpretable with accents and capitalization as the Žižkov post office. The writer says he has just visited its administrator on official business and calls him an acquaintance. The text is a claimed location and visit, not independent evidence of physical presence, employment by the postal service or a particular postal-office building. The adjective before “acquaintance” remains partly unresolved.

The following passage asks how the recipient is doing and discusses going home again in a week's time, to `usti`. Accent restoration gives Ústí, but this alone does not select one locality or establish a home address. The travel statement is qualified by `nebude-li ... odlozeno`, “unless ... is postponed.” It is a prospective conditional statement, not an accomplished journey.

The clause contains `kuratelni zalezitosli`, `meho stryce` and `uctovaci stani`. With the repair below, a possible interpretation is a curatorship matter involving the writer's uncle and an accounting-related hearing or appointment. The exact procedural meaning, tribunal, people and case are not established. The possessive `meho` needs only an accent to give “my”; no uncle's identity or civil relationship has been independently verified. No diagnosis, reason for a curatorship or legal role for the writer is inferred.

The closing anticipates the recipient's little letter and sends repeated kisses from “your Pepouš.” The familiar address and signing form do not establish legal names or common authorship with another card. The saved text does not prove that a later letter arrived or that the conditional event took place.
