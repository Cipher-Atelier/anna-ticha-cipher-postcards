# Card 247: a ball, an undelivered note and unresolved handwriting

[Anna Tichá investigation](README.md) · [All card readings](readings.md) · [Research catalogue](../README.md)

Author: Maxim Egorov. AI-assisted research and review; no external human expert authentication is claimed.

Status: partial whole-card unchanged-key reading. First replay recorded 6 October 2026 at 02:18:25 UTC; this edition was prepared later the same day. Preparation, research and public availability are distinct.

Source catalogue: [HCPortal record 1566](https://crypto.hcportal.eu/dashboard/cryptograms/1566). No complete historical date or sender identity is established.

## Abstract

The unchanged previously frozen model produces recognizably Czech affectionate correspondence. Securely supported content includes a sent kiss, a note or card that did not reach the writer, a mention of a ball, and a clause about not catching cold. The address independently names Anna Tichá. A fluent full plaintext has not been established. Several attractive completions conflict with one another or require changing fixed output letters.

The main new result is a complete source inventory and a reproducible partial reading of all four cipher rows. The experiment retains uncertain bodies, marks, spacing, overwritten ink and alternative cross-row ownership. In particular, one local source package supplies a familiar “vzpomina...” beginning but produces “vepouse...” elsewhere; another supplies “pepouse...” but only “zpomina...”. Combining the attractive portions would manufacture a reading absent from either complete package.

## Experimental design and recovery

The pre-source protocol required neutral graphical transcription, source-only reconciliation, then unchanged-key replay. On recovery, neither inherited attempt qualified as a completed independent whole-card reading: A was incomplete in its lower-row tails, and B explicitly remained an unfrozen first revision with incomplete tails and coordinate problems. Those files were preserved. A pre-key recovery amendment commissioned replacement source-only passes C and D using the original photographs and frozen neutral atlas, with no key or previous reading supplied.

C and D are separate AI reading passes, not independent external human verification. C read the permitted protocol, whose prior-exposure paragraph included earlier ordinary address/date observations. Its ordinary reading therefore is not fully unexposed. D did not read that protocol. Both passes attest that they did not access the key or other-card plaintext. The adjudicator had seen the incomplete A/B attempts and inspected the originals, so that stage is source-only adjudication rather than another independent reader.

C froze 333 candidate body slots plus ten conventional punctuation records. D froze 332 candidate body slots plus eleven punctuation records. These differences chiefly concern uncertain punctuation and whether inter-row ink constitutes an additional body. The union apparatus contains 344 record slots. It does not assert 344 physical letters. Nine disjoint joint groups explicitly preserve complete replacement packages, and an unassigned right-edge mark is recorded separately.

The recorded C and D freezes are 02:07:19 and 02:10:41 UTC on 6 October. Source adjudication froze at 02:17:04 UTC; first key access was recorded at 02:17:20 and first replay at 02:18:25. Current hashes verify those commitments and ordering records. Local timestamps and unsigned manifests do not independently prove historical access ordering or tamper-proof immutability.

The secondary frozen strictV2 rules can operate only on an originally UNKNOWN body with exactly one definite base and one total candidate, one supported exact mark geometry, and high base, shape and ownership confidence. Its acute_like_rising_stroke function is c→č, d→ď, e→ě, n→ň, r→ř, s→š, t→ť and z→ž. Its caron_V function is a→á, e→é, i→í, o→ó, u→ú and y→ý. The special G10+caron_V value is ch. These are frozen geometric operators, not an assertion that the drawn mark has its modern orthographic meaning. Dot, vague roof, compound, uncertain and unmarked-base fallback rules are not invented. No additional fixed or conditional value qualifies on this card. The primary ch from the broad G10_CAP family is a primary lookup, not a strictV2 improvement.

## Linguistic interpretation and restrained translation

A fresh final linguistic review received only the cleaned literal-and-uncertainty packet, with no key, image, glyph-family labels or earlier review supplied. It independently identified Czech. Diacritic restoration and word division can give “pusu posílá” (sends a kiss), “mně nedošel” (did not reach me), “na plese” (at the ball), “nezapomněla jsi” (you did not forget) and “nenachladila jsi se” (you did not catch cold). “Milenko”, a feminine vocative meaning beloved or sweetheart, is invariant across the listed J02 packages. The closing-like “vzpomínající a věčně tvůj” is also recognizable after accent and spacing normalization.

The following deliberately incomplete English rendering summarizes the content without supplying missing letters:

T: “[Unknown] sends a kiss and your dear [unidentified noun ending ravinku] countless times [unresolved word li...a], remembering, and forever yours, [name-like fragment pepou...].”

B1: “[Unresolved opening], beloved, [damaged expression], for the notes/cards. The [unresolved modifier] note/card did not reach me. [A damaged clause containing the masculine past form ‘had to’], and [unresolved expression] today.”

B2: “[Nonstandard literal jseh] again [unresolved place/expression]. [Package-dependent remembering-like fragment] ... how ... at the ball. [Damaged clause] me that you ... well ... . You did not forget ...”

B3: “[Damaged opening] about [package-dependent possessive and name-like phrase]. [Damaged instruction containing ‘must’, ‘for me’, ‘everything’ and ‘write out’]. You did not catch cold [question punctuation or unknown marked body]. [Unresolved closing ending pas].”

This awkwardness is evidence that a full translation is premature. The unidentified noun in T does not establish a head, cheek, hand or other body part. A nickname completion such as Pepouš is not a recovered signature and does not identify a historical sender.

Plausible completions include thanking for notes/cards; the undelivered item being the first; having to lie down again; being at an office; continual thoughts about the addressee; pleasure that she enjoyed herself; and a request for a detailed letter. These require stated unknown values or repairs and are not presented as recovered historical events or a diagnosis.

Some repairs are more consequential than adding Czech accents. The fixed output jseh cannot become jsem without changing h to m. The opening lredr[?]ha cannot become předrahá merely through diacritics. The fixed musi can be normalized to musí, but making it musíš inserts an unrepresented s. The final pas is not silently changed to a conventional closing. The full independent language report records these and other conjectures separately.

J02 is especially important. Its D package yields milenko / zpomina[?] / pepouse. Its C package yields milenko / vzpomina[?] / vepouse. Its third package retains an unknown lower body. No package supplies both the familiar vzpomín- beginning and pepouše. J01's two-body package helps the possessive svého; J03's extra lower body helps vřelý but leaves pas unexplained. Those linguistic preferences do not settle the source graph. B1–B2–B3 adjacency is plausible in places, and T looks closing-like, but no unique global traversal is adopted.

## Verification and preserved corrections

An independently written audit reconstructs all four literal rows, every conditional branch, all counts and the tagged inverse from the frozen graph and model. It does not import the original replay to generate that reconstruction. All 344 adjudicated crops and 343 D crops match their declared original RGB pixels and rotations exactly. Four recovered A region crops also match. All 343 C source-image patches reproduce from their declared cropping and resizing; they are resized derivatives, not native pixel arrays. Context windows can overlap and include neighboring-row ink. Pixel equality proves extraction fidelity, not glyph attribution or inventory completeness.

There are 316 exact tagged inverse checks, comprising 297 fixed and nineteen conditional mapped units. They establish implementation consistency and retained unit boundaries, not historical accuracy.

The audit found and retained three failures in the first implementation/process artifacts: two latent strictV2 guard bugs and two occurrences of a neutral class label in language-handoff branch names. The original guard could narrow a multi-candidate list or overwrite a conflicting base claim. Neither bug changes any actual card247 output because the affected inputs fail other gates. A separately versioned corrected guard preserves every fixed and conditional primary/strict value, family and inverse tag, and still adds zero strictV2 letters. It passes eight directed tests and a 480-case adversarial matrix. The original code and first replay remain preserved; the corrected replay is not relabelled as the first result.

The original language handoff was not fully glyph-label-free. A separately named cleaned packet was checked, and a genuinely fresh language review used it without the earlier review being supplied. The initial review is retained as preliminary evidence and is not called the fully stripped final review.

The final technical audit reports 872 passing checks and three deliberately preserved original-artifact failures. The corrected artifacts have no failures in the implemented tests. That statement does not mean perfect transcription or an externally verified decryption. No frozen source reading or model was repaired after key access.

## Conclusion

This experiment establishes a defensible whole-card source apparatus and partial unchanged-key Czech reading. Its strongest content is affectionate correspondence about an undelivered note/card, a ball, a kiss and catching cold. It does not establish one complete plaintext, an exact message length, sender identity, date or global reading order. Better source resolution or independent specialist examination of the overlapping strokes, especially J02, would be needed before promoting attractive completions into a full edition.
