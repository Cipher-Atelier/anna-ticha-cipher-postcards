## Result

The unchanged retained key gives 250 mapped positions across seven physical lines, with three unresolved definite bodies and two unresolved dotted punctuation/body units. The actual body count is 253–255: 98.04–98.81% mapping coverage, not an estimate of accuracy. All 250 mapped-class inverse checks pass; the five unknown records are not verified by re-encryption. No strict V2 additions occur. This is a new whole-card experiment in this project, not a claim of first historical solution or complete decipherment.

The text thanks the recipient for an affectionate note, promises a prompt reply, describes a probable future visit home around/by Sunday for about two days, and asks about the recipient’s stay and “svátek.” A name-day interpretation is plausible but no occasion or date is established by that word. The literal closes “tvuj pepous.” Neither that familiar signing form nor the address-form “andulko” establishes a historical identity.

## Cautious English reading

The writer warmly thanks the addressee for a charming little letter and promises to reply as soon as possible. The writer will probably have to go home around/by Sunday for about two days; this is a prospective plan, not evidence of completed travel. The following clauses ask until what date the addressee will remain in an uncertain place-form, where she will spend her “svátek,” and whether she received the writer’s little note. The place is tentatively read as v Žirovnici; svátek means a holiday/feast and could contextually refer to a name-day, but no particular date or observance is stated. The affectionate closing offers a thousand kisses and probably greetings, constant remembrance and “forever yours.” The address-form “andulko” and sign-off “pepous” do not establish anyone’s identity. These are language-level interpretations with repairs and unresolved marks, not a fully determined transcription.

## Linguistic conjectures are not source corrections

An independent language reviewer saw only the literal and stripped uncertainty metadata, with no scans, key, date, previous context or outside sources. The optional normalized reading and complete character/spacing/punctuation ledgers are in the retained research evidence. The original literal was kept byte-identical.

Material interventions are explicit:
1. zboznovama → zbožňovaná changes a known m to n (L02 literal column 22). This is not merely restored diacritics and not a decoded correction.
2. t?aviti → tráviti conjecturally fills r at L05_B011.
3. ?ozdra vu → pozdravů conjecturally fills p at L06_B010, joins a source gap and adds a diacritic.
4. L06_B043 remains unfilled. A connective a is linguistically favored and i is also possible; neither is accepted into the source or literal.
5. Both line 5 dotted units remain functionally unresolved in the normalized version, labeled Q01/Q02 rather than turned into decoded question marks.
6. Three source spaces are removed only in optional normalization: dri ve, ?ozdra vu, vzpomi najici. One space is inserted in vzirovnici → v Žirovnici. The continuation hyphen and all seven physical lines remain. No hyphen or newline is deleted.
7. Restored accents/capitalization, including tentative Žirovnici and Pepouš, are separately enumerated. The complete character ledger has 45 editorial character edits, of which exactly one changes a known base letter. These repairs do not feed back into the frozen glyph ledger.

The prospective visit is not completed travel; “about two days” is not an exact itinerary. svátek does not identify a birthday, particular name-day or feast. lístek is contextually a note/card but lexically broader. No sender’s legal name, historical person, institution, residence or actual event is established.
