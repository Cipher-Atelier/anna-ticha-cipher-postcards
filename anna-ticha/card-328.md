## Meaning and editorial limits

The opening thanks the recipient warmly for a dear note `z pelhrimova`, interpretable with capitalization and accents as “from Pelhřimov.” The writer asks whether the recipient received both notes upon returning, if U1 is filled as below, and promises a letter as soon as possible. Receipt and either person's physical location are not independently established.

A following passage, with the explicit U2 fill and `k→g` repair below, says the writer's brother visited with his own brother-in-law. It then describes departure `do kosove hory`, interpretable as “to Kosova Hora.” The Czech relationship term does not establish a more specific kinship type or identify either person. The card supplies a reported visit and trip, not independent historical verification.

The phrase `pred svatky opet prije[U3]u` concerns a possible further arrival before the holidays. Supplying `d` gives `přijedu` (“I will come”); supplying `do` gives `přijedou` (“they will come”). U3 has no accepted body count, so neither subject is established. The next question asks how long the recipient will stay for the holidays. The closing wishes many pleasant moments at home and expresses warm kisses; the terminal U4 is left unidentified.
