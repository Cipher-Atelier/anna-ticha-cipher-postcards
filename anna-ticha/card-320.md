## Literal reading

The block on [HCPortal record 1639, pc_hcp_320](https://crypto.hcportal.eu/dashboard/cryptograms/1639) gives:

```text
vrely dik a pusu za
drahy dopis, andulicko
moje rozmila. preji ti
navzajem, abys stravila
svatky v kruhu svych
drahych co nejprijemneji.
budu na te stale vzpominati.
dopis zaslu ti co nejdrive
po svatcich. nescislnekrate
te uprimne liba tvuj pepous.
```

## Meaning

The writer thanks Andulička warmly for a dear letter and sends a kiss. He reciprocates her wishes, hoping she spends the holidays as pleasantly as possible among her loved ones. He says he will keep thinking of her and send a letter as soon as possible after the holidays. The closing offers countless sincere kisses from “your Pepouš.” The printed heading *Veselé Velikonoce* identifies the card's Easter theme; the cipher itself says “holidays.”

An accent-restored display, with capitals added, is:

> Vřelý dík a pusu za drahý dopis, Anduličko moje rozmilá. Přeji ti navzájem, abys strávila svátky v kruhu svých drahých co nejpříjemněji. Budu na tě stále vzpomínati. Dopis zašlu ti co nejdříve po svátcích. Nesčíslněkráte tě upřímně líbá tvůj Pepouš.

This display preserves all accepted base letters and the older wording `rozmila`, `na te`, `vzpominati` and `nescislnekrate`; it is an editorial normalization, not a second decipherment. The final familiar-name interpretation is consistent with other card reports but does not identify a legal name or establish common authorship.

During inspection, provisional contextual guesses such as `listek`, `jedina`, `velmi` and `svatych` were rejected. The visible forms instead support `dopis`, `rozmila`, `preji` and `svatcich`. The developing Czech and key were visible throughout. These corrections are disclosed to avoid presenting the record as a blind prediction.
