## Summary

The card used to develop the substitution key appears to thank the addressee for a note, explain an unsuccessful journey, and look forward to meeting in spring.

## Editorial interpretation

The connected passages suggest a writer who had hoped to travel, could not manage it, and expects a spring meeting. The latter part appears to refer to the addressee preparing for a ball, asks to be remembered, and closes with affectionate kisses. Several names and incomplete words still resist exact reading.

The saved close ends in pepouse. Pepouše is a conventional Czech normalization of that nickname form; it is not an identification of the writer.

## Uncertainties

The displayed 336/343 coverage includes post-fit source refinements. It must not be counted as an independent test of the key.

The literal dlohych is preserved. A smooth reading as dlouhých would insert u, and no such insertion or confirmed dialect explanation is established.

The 198 inscription was rechecked after catalogue metadata had been seen; that date check was not blinded.
