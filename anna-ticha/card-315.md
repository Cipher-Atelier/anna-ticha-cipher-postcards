## Meaning

The writer thanks the recipient for a dear letter and a note, promises a prompt reply, and says he is also sending a card from Česká Třebová that he could not post on the way. He says he arrived in Prague yesterday morning and took up service today. The closing sends a thousand greetings and kisses from “your Pepouš.”

An accent-restored display, with capitals added, is:

> Vřelý dík za drahý dopis i lístek. Odpovím co nejdříve. Zasílám ti současně lístek z České Třebové, který nemohl jsem mezi cestou dáti na poštu. — Včera ráno přijel jsem do Prahy a dnes nastoupil službu. Tisíc pozdravů a pus od tvého Pepouše.

The display retains the accepted base letters, word order and older wording `mezi cestou` and `dati`. Accents and capitals are editorial. `Sluzbu` supports service or duty in a broad sense; it does not identify a military unit, profession, employer or institution. The familiar-name interpretation does not establish a legal name or common authorship with another card.

The date inscription reads `8./VI.1901`, consistent with the catalogue's 8 June 1901. Under the proposed reading, the relative arrival statement would refer to 7 June. That is an internal chronological inference from the written date and `vcera`, not an independently verified journey or postal date. The card does not establish the route, transport, duration of service or whether the accompanying item reached the recipient.
