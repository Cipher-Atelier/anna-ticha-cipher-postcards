## Meaning and editorial limits

The opening thanks “dear Anuška” for a lovely little letter or note and promises to send a letter in the next few days. This is a stated intention; the card does not demonstrate that the promised letter was later sent or received.

Taking A1 as `k`, a possible sense of the right strip is holding the recipient close in spirit and sending a thousand kisses. The distinguishing lower contour of A1 blends into the printed illustration, so syntax does not resolve the source assignment. The final two groups are retained as `pe pouc`. Interpreting them as the familiar signing form “Pepouš” requires joining the groups and replacing the accepted final `c` with `s`; it does not establish a legal name or common authorship with another card.

The left strip asks how things are going in `ka[U1]enici` and whether the recipient again has the former accommodation. “Kamenici,” the Czech locative of Kamenice, is a plausible linguistic completion if U1 supplies `m`, but that letter is not accepted from the ink. No particular settlement or address is established. The accommodation question requires the explicit repair below and does not establish a move, ownership or a tenancy.

| Literal | Possible editorial reading | Operation |

| `ka[U1]enici` | `Kamenici` | Supply `m` from unresolved ink and capitalize; not accepted. |
