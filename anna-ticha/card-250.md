## Editorial interpretation

The message wishes the addressee the best for the New Year, especially the fulfillment of her wishes. It then appears to ask whether she will love the writer in the New Year as before. A thousand kisses and vecne tvuj pepous form the affectionate signoff.

The final passage asks, in the proposed reading, whether a note was received on Sunday. The broken place expression v pel / ?rimo?e is compatible with v Pelhřimově, “in Pelhřimov,” but that identification remains an editorial completion.

## Uncertainties

The newly learned unbarred t form is absent from this card. Although the 24-entry table was applied unchanged, this card does not test that 24th assignment.

The opening noje is preserved. Reading it as moje, “my,” changes an already resolved n to m.

The affectionate address anisko does not justify silently substituting Aničko; that alternative would change s to c. An affectionate name form does not establish a historical identity.

The location, question punctuation and several words in a smooth English reading require completion or editorial normalization.
