# Source rights and publication boundary

Checked 5 October 2026 against [HCPortal’s primary terms and copyright page](https://hcportal.eu/terms.html), reached from the [portal home](https://hcportal.eu/).

The terms permit some cited noncommercial academic and personal-blog uses, while also restricting transfer/mirroring and allowing source-specific licence overrides. This review did not establish a scan-specific licence for public GitHub redistribution of cards199,440,453. We do not resolve those provisions by assuming permission.

Accordingly, this release excludes original scans, all cropped/annotated image derivatives, image-bearing PDFs, private evidence archives and any blanket licence for third-party materials. It publishes research annotations, coordinates, checksums, source links and scripts. Local image acquisition and rendering remain the reader’s responsibility under the applicable source terms. The provider’s own permissions are not assumed to transfer to this repository. The age of a postcard alone is not treated as clearance.
