## Physical evidence and limits

The intervention uses unchanged source pixels and unchanged resolution, with full-trajectory and wider focus displays, explicit physical-component allocation, fresh readings, coordinate audits and readjudication. Source review inspected all 15 target routes, all 120 significant feature fields and all 45 path-allocation entries (some referring to the same physical component across old locators). The final review accepts the complete fixed-cohort source evidence. Full construction, significant attachments, closures/openings, branches, terminal forms and competing marks are required throughout. Similarity of a common body is insufficient.

Every old alternative must remain addressed. A coarse old rectangle may contain several target and neighbor subpaths and cannot receive one owner merely from an old LEFT/RIGHT label. Old-ID target identity, same-instance aliases, detached marks and all eight feature fields remain independent eligibility gates. Source-equivalent pixels alone do not establish correct target binding or a correct scientific judgment.

The alias review includes **100 case/reference intersections involving 31 distinct unresolved IDs (30 FC references and U054)** in the [19-context geometry index](data/context-index-v1.json). These are contextual alias checks, not 100 extra glyphs or 31 resolved spans. All 135 unresolved records retain their internal partitions and statuses; none is promoted here. All 157 whole records outside the selected cohort retain baseline eligibility. The 102 exclusions outside the cohort receive no eligibility reassessment, although some can appear as neighboring context. Their irreducibility or physical information loss is not established by this bounded method.

All 15 declared full-context cited-evidence unions extend nominally beyond their old windows. This is **cited geometry only**. One approximate bbox or list of cited regions does not establish the complete necessary-evidence union; uncertainty of about four native pixels can make near-boundary containment ambiguous. Evidence cited outside an old window does not prove that adequate grounds were unavailable inside it. Wider display, allocation, new reads and readjudication vary together, so their uncontrolled before/after bundle does not isolate wider context's contribution or establish counterfactual causal necessity.

Physical path allocation assigns no historical adjunct, cipher role, letter or new occurrence. Semantic roles, historical symbol identities and letter values remain UNKNOWN. The method stops after its fixed cohort; no result generalizes to all 117 original exclusions or the remaining 102.
