## Evidence status

- **Confirmed result:** confirmation applies only to the stated check and evidence. Successful replay confirms a saved calculation, not historical accuracy. The [card 204 note](../anna-ticha/card-204.md) records an **author-reported external check**, with original text layers and twelve caveats; it does not supply public independent key replication or a complete positional package.
- **Partial reading:** a saved proposal with unresolved signs or editorial repairs, such as the [37 card reports](../anna-ticha/readings.md#preserved-card-reports).
- **Conditional analysis:** output depends on proposed source bindings, physical segmentation or a legacy key. Technical consistency does not establish historical accuracy.
- **Archived methodology:** a dated account kept unchanged. Later outcomes qualify its use without rewriting the original experiment.
