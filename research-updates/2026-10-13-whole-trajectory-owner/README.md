## What changed in the method

The target is an observed curved ink portion inside a fixed lower-landmark region, rather than an arbitrary exact stem/curve cut or one presumed tip pixel. Starting from each positively visible track, follow uninterrupted ink toward independently distinguishable upper anchors. A smooth bend is not a segmentation boundary. At every crossing, gap or junction, retain all physically admissible continuations; connected-component membership and proximity alone do not establish an owner.

Visible-portion ownership requires a positive continuous route to one anchor and no unresolved competing route that could change that owner. If all unresolved alternatives lead to the same anchor, an internal cut or exact tip may remain unknown without defeating that limited ownership result. A terminal region requires positive external-paper clearance; unavailable clearance is not feature absence. Local partition additionally requires positive paper corridors or independently traced distinct paths across the complete owner-relevant local context. A complete glyph or word still requires all constituents, neighboring ink, marks and separators to be accounted for.
