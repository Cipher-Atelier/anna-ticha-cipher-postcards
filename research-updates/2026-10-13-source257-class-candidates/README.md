## Features, marks and pair scope

The canonical signature has eight fields: path topology, enclosures, openings, stem pattern, attached bars, connected extra strokes, terminal pattern and owned-mark profile. Distinct attached bars, branches, loops or owned marks require separate variants or explicit exclusions. Detached marks are not allocated by proximity. Unresolved ownership, background confusion or uncertain full-body target blocks candidate membership, even when part of a body resembles another.

The complete pair screen has the scope of all `172 choose 2 = 14,706` unordered pairs. A metadata screen over every pair does **not** mean every pair was visually compared and confirmed. Compatible cases without an actual supported source comparison remain deferred. Signature compatibility alone does not establish a class, and transitive resemblance does not replace full membership evidence.

The checker cross-references every positive SAME outcome with the same accepted candidate class. Eligible signatures must be fully resolved and use all eight fields; a declared signature-field text difference mechanically rejects a merge. This is a comparison of saved descriptions, not a visually proven cannot-link relation. All units outside accepted classes remain explicitly accounted for. These mechanical conditions check saved declarations; separate source review is needed for whether features, mark ownership and comparisons are correct.
