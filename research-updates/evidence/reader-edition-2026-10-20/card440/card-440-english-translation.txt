24 December 1901 · editorial reading dated 20 October 2026

A thousand thanks, my treasure, for your dear letter. It [crossed in the post?] with my [note?] sent to Žirovnice. I shall be [better behaved?] [this time?] and will answer you as soon as possible.

Likewise wishing you [happy?] and merry holidays in return, and kissing you [in spirit?] […; perhaps countless times?],
forever yours,
Pepou[š?].
