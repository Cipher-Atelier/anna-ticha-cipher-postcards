## Reader-level normalization, not key changes

- Join `vzpo / ?inajiciho` as proposed *vzpomínajícího* and `?a k` as proposed *tak*. These are word-boundary/readability decisions, not extra source glyphs.
- Ordinary Czech diacritics, capitalization, commas and question marks are editorial throughout. The photographed last line does visibly end with a question mark; the reader's entire punctuation system is not claimed diplomatic.
- `míti` is an infinitive meaning *mít*. The construction *budeš mne míti … ráda* asks about continued affection. It does not mean “mother” and should not be rendered as *máti*.
- The old 24th unbarred-t assignment is not newly exercised or validated here. No fresh claim about its presence is made.
