Card 199. English reading edition
20 October 2026

This translation presents the readable text of the existing decipherment. Square brackets [candidate?] identify contextual restorations or substantive editorial repairs. The preserved literal and separate dispute ledger remain authoritative records of what is unresolved; this edition does not claim a complete solution.

Warm thanks for your dear note. I was jealous about your sleigh outing until I found out who you had gone with.

I did not go to the ball, although members of the committee came to persuade me at the very last moment, on Saturday evening. The ball turned out beautifully. There were many [ladies I knew?] [there?]. But I had no wish to amuse myself in their company. Without [you?], nothing there would have amused me.

On Monday I shall go to the Rudolfinum for the third concert of the [journalists?]. I had wanted to go to the second concert yesterday, but there were no tickets left [to buy?]. I am looking forward to this concert. The [performance?] by Ševčík’s school is said to be [magnificent?].

Pepouš, forever yours, sends a thousand [passionate?] kisses. He wishes his [sweetheart?] a good time, [but?] asks her not to forget that she belongs [exclusively?] to [Pepouš?].

Reading notes

1. The opening of the second paragraph translates the Czech editorial reading V plesu [jsem?] nebyl. The literal has vplesu sem nebyl. Separating vplesu into v plesu changes spacing only; sem → jsem proposes an additional j. That spelling normalization is recorded here rather than marking the English pronoun as [I?]: the uncertainty does not concern who is speaking. The historical preposition and inflection are not silently replaced in the Czech edition.

2. Sanici needs no letter repair. The authoritative SSJČ dictionary gives sanice the sense “ježdění na saních; sáňkování”, meaning riding on a sleigh or sled. “Sleigh outing” is a contextual translation of that attested meaning, not independent confirmation of the ciphertext reading. Vaši may refer to the whole party’s outing, while jsi addresses the recipient individually; English “your” does not preserve that distinction.

3. “Members of the committee” translates výboři, an attested older plural referring to people. It is not corrected to výbory, which would mean committees. Restoring the accents in the literal vybori does not alter its base letters.

4. The clause “Without you, nothing there would have amused me” preserves the older Czech past conditional. It does not say that the writer attended the ball and found it dull. The preceding sentence explicitly says that he did not go.

5. Contextual completions of the ten unresolved positions appear as follows:
   - [there?]: v [něm?], position 171.
   - [ladies I knew?]: [známých?] dam, position 184; “known” does not mean famous here.
   - [you?]: [tebe?], position 221.
   - [to buy?]: [ke?] koupi, positions 348–349. The unresolved signs supply the preposition ke; koupi itself is already read.
   - [performance?]: [produkce?], position 382.
   - [passionate?]: [vášnivých?], position 422.
   - [sweetheart?]: [srdéčku?], position 457; literally an affectionate “little heart”.
   - [Pepouš?] at the end: the inflected form [Pepoušovi?], positions 530 and 532. The earlier occurrence Pepouš is already mapped; the uncertainty is in this later ending, not the existence of the nickname.

6. Four further bracketed readings repair already mapped letters or insert a letter. They are not merely restorations of Czech accents:
   - curnalistu → [žurnalistů?], translated [journalists?]: c → z before restoring accents.
   - velkoleba → [velkolepá?], translated [magnificent?]: b → p.
   - avaak → [avšak?], translated [but?]: the third letter a → s before restoring the accent.
   - vyradne → [výhradně?], translated [exclusively?]: insert h between y and r before restoring accents.
   The prior source-only audit did not authorize these repairs. The original strings are preserved in the literal and dispute ledger. “Concert of the journalists” does not establish that journalists themselves performed.

7. The joining of premlou vati as přemlouvati is editorial regrouping without a change to base letters. Punctuation, capitalization, Czech accents and the narrative order of the two source regions are editorial. The frozen cross-region order remains undetermined.

8. No source-secure letter or key assignment is added by this translation. The original coverage remains 522 mapped bodies out of 532 recorded bodies, with ten unknown positions and further segmentation alternatives. Coverage is not an accuracy score. Pepouš is the writer’s self-reference in the text, not an established legal identity.
