Interpretive English translation
Interpretive English translation prepared for this contribution packet, based on the editorial Czech layer. It is not a literal translation of each disputed sign. The thousand kisses depend on the inferred M12 to S01 transition. Pepouš is retained as a nickname or signature; the historical sender is not identified. The reference to the couple above does not identify the depicted people.

Warm thanks for the dear postcard. In return, I wish my sweetheart happy and cheerful holidays. I shall think of you a great deal. I shall stay [probably in Prague] over the holidays again this year. Our Christmas separation lasts until St Stephen’s Day. I do not yet know where I shall spend Christmas Eve. I have already declined two invitations. I shall be held up at the office too late in the evening to commit myself to a particular time.

What a pity, Anička, that we cannot go to matins together like the couple above. I would give you a long kiss which, despite our both wearing coats, Anuška having a muff and me a turned-up collar, [would be] very warm. We must put it off until we meet. For now your Pepouš embraces you only in his thoughts and sends you a thousand long kisses; once again he wishes his sweetheart much happiness [at home].
